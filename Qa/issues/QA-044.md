## Copy decision
Figma mobile subtitle is the placeholder "Contact form reference" and was not used. The desktop copy (1:917) is "Have questions? Want a demo? Send us a message and our team will get back to you.", identical to what the app already shows, so the text is kept and only typography, wrapping and spacing were matched. At 12px it wraps to 2 lines (36px) instead of Figma's 1 line (18px), so the form sits 18px lower than Figma; this is the only remaining height difference and comes from the real copy.
