# QA-045: Mobile interactive banner: banner/content insets, heading offset and stat card text inset differ from Figma
- Status: fixed
- Severity: minor
- Viewport: mobile 375, 410, 420 (user-reported at 420)
- Section / Figma node: Interactive banner / 60:64, 60:65, 60:66, 60:86
- Code location: src/components/sections/FeatureTabs.tsx

## Expected (Figma, 375)
Banner 343 wide (x 17), content column x 29 / width 290 (right inset 24), heading glyph block centred in an 86px box (text starts 5.5px lower than the box top, subtitle at y 125.5), tabs 40px, left card 290x80 at y 249.5, stat card 321x179 at x 10.5 (inset 18.5 left of the content column, 12.5 to its right), stat text inset 33px (255 wide).

## Actual (app, before)
At 375 the banner was 341 wide, the content column started at 25.5 instead of 29 (3.5px), the heading was 5px higher than Figma, and the stat card text was inset 34px (card 321 wide, text 253). At 420 the stat card spanned 366 inside a 386 banner with a symmetric 15.5px overhang, so its relation to the left column differed from Figma.

## Fix
- FeatureTabs.tsx: banner `-mx-1` and `pl-[29px] pr-6` (was `-mx-[3px] px-[25.5px]`); heading `mt-[5px] mb-1.5` (+ `md:mt-0`, keeps subtitle at y 126); stat card `ml-[-18.5px] mr-[-12.5px] px-8` (was `-mx-[15.5px] px-[33px]`, 1px border now gives the Figma 33px text inset). md/xl rules unchanged.
- Measured at 375 (before -> after, Figma): banner 341 -> 343 (343); content x offset 25.5 -> 29 (29); heading y 40 -> 45 (glyph block matches 44.5); stat card 321 (321) with left overhang 10 -> 10.5; stat text width 253 -> 255 (255); heights unchanged (banner 590.5 vs 591, stat 179.5 vs 179, section 642.5 for all tabs). At 420 the stat card is 366 wide (content + 31), text 300.
- Not changed: tab widths. Figma's 108/108/74 (clipped "Colla") cannot fit 290px, so tabs stay equal-width flex (88.7 at 375, 103.7 at 420) with full labels, 40px tall, gap 12.
- Regression check: 410 (section 621.5), 768 (638), 1024 (638) and 1280 (section 434.5, stat card 487x210) unchanged.
